Buy, download, activate / 购买与激活

  1. Pay: How to get or Creem checkout ($7.99).
  2. Download: Notarized .dmg from Creem (receipt / portal).
  3. Activate: Paste the AS1.… key from the separate activation email (often within 12–24 hours). Creem’s own receipt codes are not the app key.

中文步骤见 atlassplitter.com/get/。

Seats (2 Macs) / 双机席位

  • One purchase may activate on up to 2 Macs. A second Mac may need a brief online seat claim.
  • To free a seat: Help → Deactivate This Mac (when available), or email support with your order.
  • 一笔订单最多 2 台 Mac。第二台可能需短时联网占席。释放席位：帮助 → 停用本机，或邮件联系支持并附订单信息。
