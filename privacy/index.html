Atlas Splitter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Atlas Splitter (“the App”), a macOS application, handles information.

1. Summary

Atlas Splitter is a local tool. It does not require an account, does not upload your images to our servers, and does not collect personal data for analytics or advertising.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, sell, or share:

  • Name, email, or contact lists
  • Location
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data
  • Analytics / crash reports sent to us
  • Your image contents (beyond processing on your own Mac)

3. Data processed on your device

When you use the App, image files you open are processed locally on your Mac for preview, editing, and export. Exported files are saved only to locations you choose.

The App may store local preferences and session recovery data in your Mac’s Application Support folder for the App (for example tool settings or an autosave session). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted by the App to us.

4. Internet / network

The App’s Mac App Store build is configured without network access for core features. Opening Help / Support / Privacy links may use your system browser; that is under your control.

5. Third-party / App Store

Purchases and App Store distribution are handled by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We do not operate a separate cloud backend for the App.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this Policy by posting a new version on this page and changing the “Last updated” date.

Atlas Splitter — 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年7月25日

1. 概述

Atlas Splitter 是一款 macOS 本地工具。无需账号，不会把您的图片上传到我们的服务器，也不会为分析或广告收集个人数据。

2. 我们不收集的数据

我们不收集、出售或共享：姓名/邮箱/通讯录、位置、广告标识或追踪数据、发往我们的分析/崩溃报告、以及您的图片内容（除在您本机处理外）。

3. 在您设备上处理的数据

您打开的图片仅在本机进行预览、编辑与导出；导出文件只保存到您选择的位置。

应用可能在本机 Application Support 目录保存偏好设置与会话恢复数据（如工具参数、自动会话）。这些数据留在您的电脑上，不会由本应用发送给我们。

4. 网络

Mac App Store 版本核心功能不依赖联网。打开帮助/支持/隐私链接时，可能使用系统浏览器，由您自行操作。

5. 第三方与 App Store

购买与分发由 Apple 按苹果条款与隐私政策处理。我们不为该 App 运营独立云后端。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不以儿童为目标用户，也不会故意收集儿童个人信息。

7. 变更

我们可能通过更新本页内容与「更新日期」修改本政策。
